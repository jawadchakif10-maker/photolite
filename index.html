Free online image compressor

PhotoLite helps you compress images, resize photos and convert image formats without installing an app. Select an image, adjust the quality and dimensions, choose an output format, then download the processed file. Image processing runs in your browser on your device.

Frequently asked questions

Is PhotoLite free?

Yes. PhotoLite is currently available as a free browser-based image tool.

Are my images uploaded?

The image conversion and compression process runs locally in your browser. The tool does not send your selected image to a PhotoLite image-processing server.

Which formats can I export?

You can export processed images as WebP, JPG or PNG. JPG does not preserve transparent backgrounds.

Can I resize an image?

Yes. Enter a width and height in pixels. Keep ratio is enabled by default to preserve the image proportions.

🔒 Privacy first: image processing happens locally in your browser. Your selected image is not uploaded to PhotoLite for processing.
